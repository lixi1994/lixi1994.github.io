Bio

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Alabama at Birmingham. My research interests include Multimodal AI Safety, Adversarial Machine Learning, and Reliable AI Agents. More about my research can be found in the TrustBrew Lab. I received my Ph.D. and M.S. degrees in Computer Science from Penn State University, completing my doctoral studies under the guidance of Dr. George Kesidis and Dr. David Miller. I received my B.S. degree from the School of Information Science and Engineering at Southeast University.
