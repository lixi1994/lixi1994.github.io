TrustBrew Lab ☕️

We focus on developing trustworthy and socially responsible AI systems. Our research spans the robustness, security, safety, and interpretability of AI systems, from traditional deep neural networks to modern large language models (LLMs), multimodal LLMs, and AI agents. We also explore interdisciplinary applications of trustworthy AI in critical domains, including healthcare, sustainable infrastructure, and cybersecurity.

Research Directions

  • Safety and Security of LLMs and Multimodal AI
    We study adversarial attacks, jailbreaks, and multimodal safety failures, and develop methods for failure discovery, robust learning, and proactive safeguards.
  • Trustworthy AI for Time-Series and Health Data
    We develop reliable learning methods for temporal, physiological, and longitudinal data, with applications in digital health, healthcare, and digital twins.
  • Reliable Agentic and Multimodal Reasoning
    We study reliable reasoning and interaction with multimodal data, external knowledge, and computational tools, including multi-agent and retrieval-augmented systems.
  • AI for Security and Resilient Systems
    We explore how AI can support vulnerability discovery, security analysis, and resilient decision-making in cybersecurity and critical infrastructure.
